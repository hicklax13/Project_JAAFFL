JAAFFL Design System

The Draft Room — visual language v1

A quant terminal with the poise of a private club.

JAAFFL reads your live CBS snake draft and answers one question under a running clock: who, and why. The interface has to be glanceable in a heartbeat yet dense enough to defend a pick — so the system pairs an editorial serif with a monospaced ledger of numbers, sets everything on warm ink, and spends its one bold note on brass.

01Palette

Neutrals are warm, not grey — a bias toward the brass so the ground reads chosen. Brass is the single accent, reserved for the recommended pick and primary affordances. Status and position hues are distinct, reserved roles. Each swatch adapts to the active theme; both hexes are listed (dark / light).

02Typography

Three voices: a transitional serif for display and player names, a humanist sans for interface text, and a true monospace for every number — scores, survival %, projections — so figures align and read like a ledger. Type stays on a modular scale; numbers use tabular figures.

05Chart marks & legend

Data marks follow one spec across every surface: thin 2px lines, rounded data-ends anchored to a baseline, ≥8px markers, recessive grid, emphasized endpoints, and a legend whenever two or more series share a frame. Identity is never color-alone — position hues sit beside their letter or a direct label.
